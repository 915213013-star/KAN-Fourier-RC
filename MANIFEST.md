# Release manifest

This is a code-only release assembled from the research workspace.

## Included

- model definitions and training routines for KAN-Fourier and IQCC-Former;
- train-split OOF generation;
- HCS, Pairwise, and GAMC-inspired auxiliary predictors;
- ERU estimation and validation-stable correction selection;
- component-ablation, complexity, comparison, t-SNE, and paper-figure tools;
- portable PowerShell launchers and documentation.

## Deliberately excluded

- `RML2016.10a_dict.pkl`, `RML2016.10b.dat`, and extracted dataset trees;
- all `*.pth`, `*.npz`, `*.npy`, `*.joblib`, and large feature caches;
- historical search scripts, failed probes, logs, transfer archives, and paper drafts;
- personal machine paths and environment-specific interpreter paths.

Empty tracked directories indicate where local artifacts are created.
